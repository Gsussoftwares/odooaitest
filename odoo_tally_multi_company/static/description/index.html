Extension Addon Requires: odoo_tally_connector

Tally Multi-Company Connector

Connect and synchronize to multiple Tally Prime companies simultaneously across multiple ports or network endpoints with automatic partner-to-company destination routing.

Figure 1: Centralized Tally Companies Dashboard with Live Connection Status Badges

Key Multi-Company Capabilities

Simultaneous Multi-Endpoint Management
Configure unlimited Tally Prime company endpoints on different ports (e.g. 9000, 9001) on the same machine or across different LAN IP addresses and VPNs.
Automatic Partner-to-Company Routing
Assign Customers and Vendors to specific Tally companies. Invoices, bills, and payments automatically route to the corresponding destination company.
Live Per-Company Connection Testing
Test connectivity directly on each Tally company form. Status badges and ribbon banners immediately reflect connected or failed state.
